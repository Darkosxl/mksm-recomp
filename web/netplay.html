Controls

Gamepad: plug it in and press any button on it. Standard layout: A/✕, B/○, X/□, Y/△, bumpers L1/R1, triggers L2/R2, sticks, Start, Select/Back.

Keyboard: WASD left stick, ←↑→↓ d-pad, Space ✕, Esc ○, J □, K △, Q L1, C R1, Shift L2, E R2, Enter Start, Backspace Select. Double-click the picture for fullscreen.
